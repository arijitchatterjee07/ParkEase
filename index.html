ParkEase NCR Strategy & Pan-India Rollout Plan

Why we start hyper-local in New Delhi & Haryana before scaling across India

Why Start With New Delhi & Haryana Only?

  • Micro-Market Supply Density: An empty map across 28 states kills trust. Concentrating 250+ verified spots in Saket, CP, AIIMS (Delhi) and Cyber Hub, Medanta, Faridabad (Haryana) guarantees high fill rates.
  • Cross-Border Daily Commuters: Thousands of daily commuters travel between Delhi and Gurugram/Faridabad—one unified wallet-free QR pass serves both ends of their commute.
  • Long-Tail Supply Moat: Targets Delhi builder-floor stilts, RWA visitor slots, and Gurugram clinic frontages instead of fighting Park+ on gated malls.
  • Honest Confidence Labels: Separates Live Hardware, Reserved Quota, and Estimated spots to eliminate "arrived to a full lot" churn.
